Privacy Policy

Bible Adventures: Walk in Faith — Last updated: August 14, 2026

Bible Adventures: Walk in Faith ("the app") is a children's Bible storybook game. This policy explains, plainly, what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

The app does not collect any personal information, from children or anyone else. It has no account or sign-in, works fully offline, contains no ads, no in-app purchases, and no chat or messaging with other people.

Information we collect

None. The app never asks for a child's name, email address, phone number, or location, and never requests access to contacts, the camera, or the microphone. The Android app declares zero permissions in its manifest — it has no technical ability to access any of these, not just a policy of not asking.

How progress is saved

All game progress (character choices, completed chapters, earned badges) is saved in a single file stored locally on the device. Nothing is uploaded, synced, or sent anywhere else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes this file completely.

Third parties

The app includes no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no other third-party tracking or data-collection libraries of any kind. It makes no network requests at all — it works the same with the device fully offline.

Children's privacy

This app is designed for children ages 7 and up and is built from the ground up to collect nothing from any user, child or adult, in compliance with the intent of the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. Because no personal information is ever collected, there is no information to review, correct, or delete on behalf of a child — there simply isn't any stored anywhere but on the device itself.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, an updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "Last updated" date above.
