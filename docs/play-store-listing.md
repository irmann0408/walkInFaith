## Full description (≤4000 characters)

```
Walk through the Bible's best-loved stories as a gentle, story-driven adventure built just for kids.

Bible Adventures: Walk in Faith takes children ages 7+ through 8 classic Bible stories — Noah's Ark, David and Goliath, The Good Samaritan, Daniel and the Lions, Esther's Rescue of Her People, The Battle of Jericho, Feeding the 5,000, and Jesus Calms the Storm — each one a full story-to-scripture adventure: story intro, exploration, gentle puzzles, a lesson, and a real scripture verse to close it out.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Every challenge is built from helping, matching, counting, and finding — never fighting, never racing a punishing clock, and there's no way to "lose." A wrong answer just means "try again," so kids can explore at their own pace without frustration or pressure.

FEATURES

• 8 complete Bible story adventures, unlocked one at a time as your child completes each one
• Gentle mini-games: matching pairs, drag-and-drop sorting, hidden-object searches, simple counting and math puzzles, and calm rhythm-tap challenges — no combat, ever
• A customizable character (appearance, hairstyle, skin tone, and outfit color) that travels through every story
• Collectible badges and real scripture cards (World English Bible) earned by completing each adventure
• A Parent Area with playtime stats and progress at a glance
• Fully offline — no account, no sign-in, no internet connection required to play

BUILT FOR PEACE OF MIND

• No ads, ever
• No in-app purchases
• No chat, messaging, or any way to contact other people
• No personal information collected — nothing to sign up for, nothing to type in
• No data leaves the device — everything is saved in one local file, only on the device it's installed on

Bible Adventures: Walk in Faith is designed to be something you can hand to your child and walk away from — warm, unhurried, and safe by design, not just by promise.
```
(character count: comfortably under 4000, roughly 1,750)

## Target age / content rating questionnaire

Answer based on the app's actual content — no violence, no fear content,
no user-generated content, no unrestricted internet access, no in-app
purchases, no ads, no gambling, no profanity, no references to
drugs/alcohol/tobacco. This should produce the lowest available content
rating (e.g. "Everyone" / PEGI 3, depending on region). Target age group:
**7 and up** (matches the app's actual design target — not "5 and under,"
since some puzzles involve real reading and simple arithmetic).

## Data Safety form

Answer **"No data collected"** for every category Play Console asks
about (personal info, financial info, location, messages, photos/videos,
app activity, device IDs, etc.) — this matches reality: the app's
`AndroidManifest.xml` requests zero permissions, makes no network calls,
and the Privacy Policy (https://irmann0408.github.io/walkInFaith/privacy-policy.html)
states this explicitly. There is nothing to declare as shared with third
parties, since there are no third-party SDKs in the app at all.
